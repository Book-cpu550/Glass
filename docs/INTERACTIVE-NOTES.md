# Interactive notes in Glass

Everything below runs locally except collaboration, which uses the configured encrypted sharing relay. Existing notes and PDF exports retain still images for interactive figures.

- **Insert at the cursor:** type `/` at the start of a paragraph or after a space. Search for equations, tables, symbols, graphs, molecules, biology diagrams, or drawings. Ordinary slashes inside words and equations keep their usual meaning.
- **Command search:** press **Ctrl K**, or choose **Insert / Commands**. Use Up/Down and Enter to choose an action; Escape closes it.
- **Outline and folding:** choose **Outline** to jump between headings. Use the triangle beside a heading to fold its section. Folding is a local view setting; PDF exports still include every section.
- **Focus:** choose **Focus mode** or press **Ctrl Shift F**. The sidebar and editing bars disappear. The small dock offers Insert / Commands and Exit focus. Selecting prose reveals nearby formatting controls, font-size adjustment, an equation action, and a flashcard action. Times New Roman stays the default.
- **Equation sliders:** open **Draw → Math graphing → Equation sliders & linked equation**. Add sliders named a, b, etc.; set their bounds and step, then write an equation such as `y=a*sin(b*x)`. Changing a slider updates the plot. After inserting it, choose **Interact with graph** to adjust sliders in the note. **Save view** keeps the settings and updates the still image.
- **Linked equations:** in the same panel, choose a graphable equation from the current note. The active interactive graph follows edits to that equation. Save view updates its static image for notes and PDF export. Supported graph expressions use y=f(x), r=f(theta), or z=f(x,y); unsupported notation gives an error and preserves the previous image. If a linked source is copied or removed, choose the intended source again.
- **Inspect a graph:** Cartesian graphs offer point coordinates, slope, roots, intersections of the first two equations, and signed area over an interval. In an interactive note graph, click a curve or enter X and choose Inspect point & interval. These are numerical estimates; tangent roots, narrow features, and discontinuities can require closer inspection.
- **Motion playback:** physics trajectory plots in the drawing window and in notes offer **Play/Pause**, **Restart motion**, and a time slider. The object follows the primary curve; velocity and net-force direction arrows are normalized for visibility, with actual values in the readout. Both 2D and 3D motion work.
- **Biology exploration:** insert a biology diagram, then choose **Explore biology diagram**. Click a structure for an explanation, add your own explanation in edit mode, or use **Hide labels / Quiz** and Reveal structure. Annotations are stored in the local editable diagram; the original labeled image remains available for PDF output.
- **Molecule exploration:** insert a generated 3D molecule and choose **Interact with molecule**. Rotate, pan, and zoom; select atoms to inspect identity and connected bonds. Two selected atoms give distance; three give an angle with the middle atom as vertex. Measurements use a generated conformer and are approximate, not experimental measurements.
- **Flashcards:** select prose, an equation, or a diagram and choose **Flashcard** in the selection toolbar or command menu. Enter a question and answer, then Save card. **Flashcards** offers due/all reviews, answer reveal, Again/Good/Easy scheduling, editing, and removal. Cards and progress stay with the local note and are included in notebook backups; they are not sent to collaborators.
- **Collaboration feedback:** shared notes show participants, colored cursors/selections, connection state, and pending delivery. “Synced to sharing service” confirms the relay echoed the stored encrypted update; it does not promise that every collaborator is online. Offline edits remain local and merge after reconnection.

Only one graph viewer stays active at a time to limit graphics-memory use. Close it with Done. Saving a view requires edit access; read-only viewers can explore without modifying the document. Ink remains in the saved still image when a graph view is updated.
